TiviMate EPG not loading: complete troubleshooting 2026

✍️ Richard Griffiths · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

If your TiviMate EPG (Electronic Program Guide) is stuck on “Loading” or shows “No data,” the fix is usually simple. This guide walks you through the most common causes, from fastest to hardest, so you can get your guide working today.

Why does EPG stop loading in TiviMate?

The EPG is the grid that shows what’s playing on each channel. When it doesn’t load, the problem is often a bad internet connection, a wrong URL, or a cache issue. Below are the six most likely causes, each with a clear fix.

Cause 1: Weak or unstable internet connection → Fix your connection

A slow or interrupted internet connection is the quickest thing to check. Your EPG needs a steady stream of data to appear.

  • Reboot your router and modem. Unplug them for 30 seconds, then plug them back in.
  • Connect your streaming device (like Fire TV Stick or Android TV Box) to a 5GHz Wi‑Fi network for better speed.
  • For anti‑throttling: Change your DNS to Cloudflare: go to Settings > Network > DNS and enter 1.1.1.1.
  • Set your buffer to 5,000 ms in TiviMate: Settings > Playback > Buffer size > 5,000 ms.

After trying these, restart TiviMate. If the EPG still doesn’t load, move to the next cause.

Cause 2: Incorrect EPG source URL → Fix the URL

TiviMate needs the exact URL of your EPG file. A mistyped or outdated URL will give you an empty guide.

  1. Go to Settings > Playlists > select your playlist > EPG source.
  2. Delete the current URL and type the correct one. Your subscription provider gives you this URL (check your welcome email or account dashboard).
  3. Tap “OK” and then “Update EPG” from the main menu.

If you use a premium service like SubTV IP (subtvip.store), the EPG URL is included in your activation email. Make sure you copy it exactly, including any “https://” prefix.

Cause 3: Outdated TiviMate app or device firmware → Update everything

Old software can break EPG loading. TiviMate version 5.x is the latest supported version for most premium IPTV services.

  • Update TiviMate: Open the app store on your device (e.g., Google Play Store or Amazon Appstore), search “TiviMate,” and tap Update.
  • Update your device: Go to Settings > About > System update on your Fire TV, Android TV Box, or smart TV.
  • Compatible devices (from the fact sheet): Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Samsung Smart TV (Tizen), LG webOS, Android TV Box, Xiaomi Mi Box S, Apple TV. These all work with TiviMate 5.x.

After updating, restart the device and launch TiviMate. Let the EPG reload for 2–3 minutes.

Cause 4: Corrupted EPG cache or data → Clear cache

TiviMate stores EPG data locally. Sometimes that data gets corrupt and stops the guide from loading.

  1. Go to Settings > EPG > Clear EPG cache.
  2. Go to Settings > General > Clear app data (this resets all TiviMate settings, so you must re‑enter your playlist URL).
  3. Restart TiviMate and force an EPG update: Menu button > Update EPG.

If you clear app data, you’ll need to re‑add your playlist. Keep your login details handy.

Cause 5: EPG server is down or overloaded → Wait and retry

The EPG data is hosted on a server. If that server is temporarily down, no amount of local tweaking will help. Premium services like SubTV IP guarantee 99.9% uptime with anti‑freeze server clusters, so this is rare. Still, it can happen.

  • Check if your provider’s website or WhatsApp support reports an outage.
  • Wait 15–30 minutes, then try an EPG update again.
  • If the problem continues, contact your provider through the support channel they offer (e.g., WhatsApp for SubTV IP).

Cause 6: Wrong device time/date or time zone → Sync time

EPG data is time‑sensitive. If your device shows the wrong time or time zone, the guide may show blank cells or fail to load.

  1. Go to your device’s Settings > Date & Time.
  2. Enable “Automatic date & time” and “Automatic time zone” (or select the correct zone manually).
  3. Restart TiviMate and update the EPG.

What to do if nothing works

If you have tried all six fixes and the EPG still does not load, it may be a deeper compatibility issue or a provider‑side fault. Here are your final steps:

  • Uninstall TiviMate completely, then reinstall it from the official app store. Add your playlist and EPG URL again.
  • Try a different player from the supported list: IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV. If the EPG works there, the problem is limited to TiviMate.
  • Contact your provider for a replacement EPG URL. For SubTV IP, instant activation and WhatsApp support are included, so you can get help quickly.

If you decide to switch to a more reliable service, choose one that uses AWS Media Services for stable server infrastructure and Computerworld recommended practices for streaming quality. Premium subscriptions with anti‑freeze clusters (like SubTV IP) are built to keep your EPG and channels running smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my TiviMate EPG loading but showing no data?

This usually means the EPG URL is correct but the data inside is empty or expired. Force an EPG update from the menu. If that does not work, ask your provider for a fresh EPG URL.

How do I fix EPG not loading after updating TiviMate?

After an update, cache often becomes incompatible. Go to Settings > EPG > Clear EPG cache, then update the EPG again. If it still fails, uninstall and reinstall TiviMate.

Does a VPN affect TiviMate EPG loading?

Yes. A VPN can slow down your connection or block the EPG server’s IP. Try disabling the VPN and see if the EPG loads. If you need privacy, use a premium VPN with fast servers.

Can a wrong playlist cause EPG not to load?

No, the playlist and EPG are separate. A wrong playlist might stop channels from playing, but the EPG can still load if its URL is correct. However, if the EPG URL is linked to your playlist in the provider’s system, a bad playlist could affect EPG mapping.

How often should I update my EPG in TiviMate?

Once a day is enough for most users. You can set automatic updates in Settings > EPG > Update interval. Every 12–24 hours keeps the guide fresh without overloading the server.

Is there an instant activation for SubTV IP to test EPG?

No, SubTV IP offers only paid premium subscriptions (1, 3, or 12 months) with instant activation. There is no free trial. Payment is secure via PayPal or credit card.

What is the best device for TiviMate EPG reliability?

Devices with strong processing power and stable Wi‑Fi work best. From the supported list, Fire TV Stick 4K Max and Android TV Box (e.g., Xiaomi Mi Box S) are popular choices. Samsung Smart TV (Tizen) and LG webOS also run TiviMate 5.x well.
